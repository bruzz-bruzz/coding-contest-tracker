# Privacy Policy for Coding Contest Tracker

**Effective Date:** August 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to the Coding Contest Tracker, a Discord bot designed to provide users with information about upcoming coding contests. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal information when you use our bot. The bot is developed and operated by bruzz-bruzz.

## 2. Information We Collect
### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Discord User ID:** When you interact with the bot, we collect your Discord User ID to identify your account and provide personalized contest notifications.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Interaction Data:** We automatically collect data about your interactions with the bot, such as the commands you use and the times you interact with it.
- **Server Data:** If you use the bot in a server, we may collect information about the server, such as the server ID and the channels where the bot is active.

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties
- We do not collect any information from third parties.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
- **Provide Services:** To provide you with contest notifications and other features.
- **Improve Services:** To understand how the bot is used and to improve its functionality.
- **Communicate with You:** To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support.

## 4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services
- **Discord API:** We use the Discord API to interact with the Discord platform. Discord may collect certain information about you, as outlined in their privacy policy.
- **Analytics:** We do not use any third-party analytics services.

## 5. Data Retention
- We retain your Discord User ID and interaction data for as long as you continue to use the bot. If you request the deletion of your data, we will remove it from our systems within a reasonable time frame.

## 6. Data Security
- We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Your Rights
### 7.1 Rights Under GDPR (EU Users)
- **Access:** You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you.
- **Correction:** You have the right to correct any inaccuracies in your personal information.
- **Deletion:** You have the right to request the deletion of your personal information.
- **Portability:** You have the right to receive a copy of your personal information in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
- **Opt-Out:** You have the right to object to the processing of your personal information for certain purposes.

### 7.2 Rights Under CCPA (California Users)
- **Access:** You have the right to request the categories and specific pieces of personal information we collect about you.
- **Deletion:** You have the right to request the deletion of your personal information.
- **Opt-Out:** You have the right to opt out of the sale of your personal information, though we do not sell personal information.

## 8. Children's Privacy
- Our bot is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 9. Cookies and Tracking Technologies
- We do not use cookies or other tracking technologies to collect information from users.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy
- We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the updated policy on our website and revising the effective date.
